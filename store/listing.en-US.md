## Description
Kirapix is a fast, easy photo viewer and editor for Windows. View, organize and edit your pictures in one app.

INSTANT VIEWING
Double-click a picture and the lightweight Kirapix Quick opens in about half a second. Press ← / → to flip through the folder instantly. Nearby images are preloaded in the background, so there is almost no waiting. Double-click the picture to open the full organizer and editor.

ORGANIZE
Folder tree, quick access, large thumbnails with a disk cache, EXIF details (camera, shutter, aperture, ISO, GPS), star ratings with filtering, sorting and search, drag-and-drop move or copy, batch rename, and batch resize and convert.

NON-DESTRUCTIVE EDITING
Exposure, brightness, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, clarity and dehaze. Levels and tone curves, white balance with an eyedropper, vibrance and saturation, HSL color EQ, color balance, split toning and black & white. Sharpening and noise reduction, vignette and film grain, plus presets. Every change can be undone, and the original is backed up before saving.

CROP, RESIZE, TEXT, STITCH
Crop freely or to a fixed ratio. Resize by pixels, percentage or common long sides. Add text to photos with your choice of font, color, outline, shadow, background box and rotation. Stitch several photos into one image, downward, to the right or to the left.

MORE
Lossless JPEG rotation, animated GIFs, full screen and slideshow, and adjustable interface font size. Works offline and collects no personal data.

Kirapix is completely free with no ads, and every feature is included.

Interface language: Traditional Chinese.
